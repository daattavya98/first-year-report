\chapter{Progress Report}
\label{chap:progress}
As summarized in the preceeding chapters, I would like to roughly organize my PhD thesis into the following components:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Theory}:
        \subitem \textit{The nature of mathematical discovery, particularly in the age of AI}
        \subitem \textit{``Interestingness'' and the conjecture space}
        \subitem \textit{Multi-agent system based mathematical discovery}
    \item \textbf{Applications}:
        \subitem \textit{Domain specific applications: Holographic entropy inequalities, $\text{WAM}(n,s)$ and the $\text{abc}$-conjecture}
        \subitem \textit{Conjectures as sub-goals in proof formalization} 
\end{itemize}
In the remainder of this chapter, I will summarize the course of action and present a timeline for the completion of the PhD.

\section{Course of action}
The overarching theme in the second year of the PhD will be to incorporate the insights gained from the applications I have worked on in the first year
and utilise those to improve the theoretical framework. Simultaneously, I intend to have a greater focus on literature specific to multi-agent systems, mathematical discovery and formalisation. This is a direction that has only become clear towards the end of the first year of the PhD. It still requires groundwork and a proof-of-concept to connect with the rest of the ideas presented in this report.
\\ In the third year, the aim is to discover new mathematics and work on follow-ups to previous applications. Ideally, I will be finishing ongoing publications by the end of the third year.
